What you are looking at

Grey is steel, copper-coloured blocks are coils, light grey is aluminium, the tan disk is the plastic insulator that holds the gun's high-voltage parts away from everything else, and red is the part that sits at up to 15,000 volts. The sample surface is at the origin and the gun is at the top.

  • Whole instrument. Everything stacked up, for seeing how it fits together. The two lenses, the beam tube, the gun and the coil packs are the real first-pass shapes. The chamber, stage, sample stub and turbo pump are placeholders of roughly the right size.
  • Objective lens. The lens nearest the sample. Three turned steel parts around a coil, with a wide bore in the top for the scan coils and a tapered nose underneath.
  • Condenser lens. The upper lens, which does the first shrink of the beam. Same three-part construction with a flat lower pole.

These are preliminary shapes, correct for the magnetic design but not yet ready to machine: bolt holes, locating registers, wire exits and seals are still to be added.
